BSCC Constitution

ARTICLE I — NAME

The name of the organization shall be the “Business Strategy & Communications Club” (BSCC).

ARTICLE II — PURPOSE

The purpose of the organization shall be to expose students to business communication, decision-making, and psychology, and to sharpen their soft skills. Many students do not have the communication skills that come naturally to others. This club hopes to train and hone those skills to teach students how to read and analyze body language, train their speech to communicate better, discuss the importance of business communication, and come up with strategies that allow them to leave impactful impressions that showcase their skills and merits.

Section I: BSCC will hold events at least twice a month including Lecture: Analyzing Body Language, Communication Night, and “What Would YOU Do?” Game Show Night.

ARTICLE III — MEMBERSHIP

Membership shall be open to all Rutgers University students and must comply with federal laws prohibiting discrimination on the basis of race, religion, color, national origin, ancestry, age, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, disability, marital status, military service, and veteran status.

ARTICLE IV — EXECUTIVE BOARD OFFICERS

The officers of the Executive Board shall be President, Vice-President, Treasurer, Secretary, and Marketing Head.

  • Section I (President): Responsible for overseeing organizational meetings, goals, and official representation.
  • Section II (Treasurer): Responsible for financial transactions, current reports, and joint approvals.
  • Section III (Vice President): Serves in presidential absence, chairs committees, oversees recruitment and retention.
  • Section IV (Secretary): Records activities, maintains official university correspondence, keeps minutes.
  • Section V (Marketing Head): Promotes events and social media via physical, social, and word-of-mouth media.

ARTICLE V — ELECTIONS

Elections will take place by Spring Break by secret ballot majority vote. Nominations precede elections by approximately one week. Candidates caught attempting to unfairly siphon votes will be removed from candidacy.

ARTICLE VI — IMPEACHMENT

Brought by any 3 members on grounds of negligence or severe infraction. Requires two-thirds majority vote of voting members.

ARTICLE VII — AMENDMENTS

Proposed in writing 1 week prior to vote. Requires 2/3 majority vote subject to Division of Student Affairs approval.

ARTICLE VIII — VOTING RIGHTS

Active membership/voting rights require attendance at a minimum of 3 BSCC events/meetings before spring elections.

ARTICLE IX & X — COMPLIANCE

Abides by all applicable laws of the State of New Jersey, United States, and rules of Rutgers University Division of Student Affairs.
