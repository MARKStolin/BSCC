BSCC Constitution

ARTICLE I: NAME

The name of the organization shall be the “Business Strategy & Communications Club” (BSCC).

ARTICLE II: PURPOSE & PROGRAMMING

The purpose of the organization shall be to expose students to business communication, decision-making, and psychology, and to sharpen their soft skills...

Section I: BSCC will hold events at least twice a month, including:

  • Lecture: Analyzing Body Language: Faculty-led sessions on non-verbal communication and Q&A.
  • Communication Nights: Interactive mock interviews, watercooler talks, and business deal stations.
  • “What Would YOU Do?” Game Show Night: Team-based strategic scenario competition.

ARTICLE III: MEMBERSHIP

Open to all Rutgers University students complying with federal non-discrimination standards...

ARTICLE IV: EXECUTIVE BOARD

Officers consist of President, Vice-President, Treasurer, Secretary, and Marketing Head.

ARTICLE V: ELECTIONS

Elections occur annually by Spring Break by secret ballot majority vote.

ARTICLE VIII: VOTING RIGHTS

Active membership & voting rights require attendance at a minimum of 3 BSCC events/meetings prior to spring elections.
