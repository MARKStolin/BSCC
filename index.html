BSCC Constitution

ARTICLE I — NAME

The name of the organization shall be the “Business Strategy & Communications Club” (BSCC).

ARTICLE II — PURPOSE & PROGRAMMING

The purpose of the organization shall be to expose students to business communication, decision-making, and psychology, and to sharpen their soft skills. Many students do not have the communication skills that come naturally to others. This club hopes to train and hone those skills to teach students how to read and analyze body language, train their speech to communicate better, discuss the importance of business communication, and come up with strategies that allow them to leave impactful impressions that showcase their skills and merits.

ARTICLE III — MEMBERSHIP

Membership shall be open to all Rutgers University students complying with university guidelines and non-discrimination laws.

ARTICLE IV — EXECUTIVE BOARD OFFICERS

The officers of the Executive Board shall be President, Vice-President, Treasurer, Secretary, and Marketing Head.

ARTICLE V — ELECTIONS

Elections take place by Spring Break by secret ballot. Active membership/voting rights require attendance at a minimum of 3 BSCC events/meetings prior to elections.
